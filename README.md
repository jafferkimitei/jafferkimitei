### About Me

I'm a Solutions Architect and Full Stack Engineer focused on designing and delivering end-to-end digital systems.

I work across the entire product lifecycle, from understanding business requirements and defining technical architecture to designing user experiences, engineering applications, integrating services, and deploying production-ready systems.

My approach combines architectural thinking, software engineering, product design, and infrastructure automation to transform complex requirements into reliable, maintainable, and scalable solutions.

### Architecture & Engineering

- **Solution Architecture:** System design, technical architecture, requirements analysis, architectural decisions, and scalable application structures.
- **Backend & API Architecture:** Service design, REST APIs, authentication, integrations, business logic, and data management.
- **Frontend Engineering:** Modern web applications, reusable component systems, responsive interfaces, and accessibility.
- **Data Architecture:** Relational and NoSQL databases, schema design, data modelling, caching, and performance optimization.
- **AI Integration:** LLM-powered applications, intelligent workflows, AI provider integrations, and automation.
- **DevOps & Infrastructure:** Docker, Jenkins, CI/CD pipelines, GitHub automation, server deployments, environment management, monitoring, and operational reliability.
- **Product & UX Design:** User journeys, system workflows, prototyping, design systems, and human-centred product experiences.

### Systems I Build

My engineering work spans several areas of digital infrastructure and business technology:

- **SaaS Platforms:** Multi-functional applications, administration systems, dashboards, and intelligent business workflows.
- **E-commerce & Commerce Infrastructure:** Product catalogues, inventory management, order processing, checkout, payments, and financial workflows.
- **Payment & Transaction Systems:** Payment gateway integrations, M-Pesa workflows, transaction tracking, and payment reconciliation.
- **Event Technology:** Digital ticketing, QR-based ticket validation, event management, and analytics.
- **AI-Powered Applications:** Intelligent assistants, AI integrations, automated processes, and provider-independent AI architectures.
- **Identity & Information Systems:** QR-enabled identification, lost-and-found workflows, and connected digital services.

### Engineering Philosophy

I believe good architecture is more than choosing a technology stack. It is about understanding the problem, making sound technical decisions, designing for change, and building systems that remain reliable as they grow.

My focus is on:

- Architecture before implementation.
- Maintainability over unnecessary complexity.
- Security, accessibility, and performance by design.
- Automation and repeatable deployment processes.
- Technology decisions aligned with real business objectives.
